# Feedback — dissertation draft `template 6` (24 September 2026)

All of the 23 September list is in. This round is different: two new measurements
change numbers and claims that come from the FGSM side of the work, not from your
experiments. **Your learned-adversary results are unaffected** — it never uses the
victim's gradients, so neither problem below touches it. What changes is the
baseline you compare against and the ceiling you measure against.

The two findings, briefly:

1. **The ~21 pp damage ceiling is CC-Simple's, not every victim's.** Each victim has
   its own ceiling (clean delivery minus delivery with every decision forced onto
   the most congested path, load 2.0):

   | Variant | Ceiling (pp) |
   |---|---|
   | CC-Simple | 21.4 |
   | CC-Duelling | 16.5 |
   | CC-Simple-GNN | 14.7 |
   | CC-Duelling-GNN | 14.4 |
   | LC-Simple | 11.3 |
   | LC-Duelling | 10.8 |
   | LC-Duelling-GNN | 7.3 |

   Your largest gap, 5.90 pp on LC-Simple, is therefore **about half** of what was
   available on that victim, not under a third. The largest gap (1.45 pp,
   LC-Simple, b = 0.25) is 13%, not under a tenth. *This error was from FGSM work: it came
   from item 4 of the previous feedback.*

2. **FGSM was a weaker baseline than we thought, for two separate reasons.**
   - *Gradient masking.* The victim's chosen outputs are saturated (σ > 0.99 in
     82–96% of decisions), so FGSM's gradient, taken through those sigmoid
     outputs, nearly vanishes. A single-step attack on the pre-sigmoid logits,
     same ε and same 15 episodes, does more damage than FGSM on three of the seven
     victims: CC-Duelling **+3.28 pp more [+1.76, +4.80]**, CC-Duelling-GNN
     **+2.06 pp more [+1.03, +3.10]** and LC-Duelling-GNN +0.91 pp more
     [+0.32, +1.51]. On LC-Simple and LC-Duelling it does *less* (−3.48 and
     −2.38 pp, both significant).
   - *The GNN attack was aimed at the wrong function.* For the three GNN variants
     the FGSM code took its gradient through the actor alone, bypassing the
     encoder, while the victim decides through the encoder. It did **not** zero the
     other agents' observations, as §3.2.2 says: that code path was never
     reached. Re-run through the victim's real decision path, the GNN rows of
     Table 4.5 change (item 3).
